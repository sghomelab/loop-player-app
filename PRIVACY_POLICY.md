# Loop Deck Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 31, 2026

## Overview

Loop Deck ("the App") is a local audio loop player. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

## Data We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

Loop Deck is designed to work entirely on your device. All audio files you import are processed locally and never leave your phone. We have no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

## What the App Does With Your Data

- **Audio files:** You import audio files from your device's storage. These files are read locally for playback and looping. They are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any server.
- **Loop settings:** Your saved A/B loop points, repeat counts, and preferences are stored locally on your device in app-specific storage.
- **App preferences:** Your chosen color theme, playback speed, and other settings are stored locally.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- No account creation required
- No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
- No location data
- No usage analytics or crash reporting
- No advertising identifiers
- No third-party data sharing

## Children's Privacy

Loop Deck does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13. The App is suitable for all ages.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted, there is no risk of data breach on our end. Your device's own security measures (passcode, encryption) protect your data.

## Data Deletion

To delete your data, simply uninstall the App. This removes all locally stored loop settings, preferences, and any cached audio data. No action is needed on our part because we hold no data.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
